# 🎧 MoodTunes

**Your mood. Your moment. Your music.**

MoodTunes is an AI-powered music recommendation app that helps you discover songs based on your mood, situation, and musical preferences. Simply describe how you're feeling, and get recommendations that fit the moment.

**🌐 Live Demo:** https://moodtunes.shanhui2428.workers.dev/

## 💡 About the Project

I noticed a pattern in my own listening habits: I often return to the same songs, even when they don't match how I'm feeling. Finding the right song for a specific mood takes more effort than it should.

That observation inspired MoodTunes — an attempt to make music discovery more personal, intuitive, and effortless.

## ✨ Features

- **Mood-Based Recommendations:** Describe your mood or situation in natural language.
- **Personalized Suggestions:** Get around 10 song recommendations with explanations of why each song fits your mood.
- **Custom Filters:** Filter songs by language, genre, era, energy level, and vocal or instrumental preference.
- **Discover Mode:** Explore new music beyond your usual listening habits.
- **My Music Mode:** Discover recommendations based on your musical taste.
- **User Feedback:** Favorite songs, give thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback, regenerate recommendations, or use the "Surprise Me" option.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **AI:** Large Language Model (LLM) for mood analysis and recommendation generation
